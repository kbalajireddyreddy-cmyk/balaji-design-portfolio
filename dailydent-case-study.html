Problem Statement

Dentists and patients lack a strong channel for talking to each other. Patients hesitate over treatment because they don't understand the procedures; dentists struggle to explain plans and their complexities. Care after treatment suffers as a result — lower treatment compliance, higher risk of complications, and less satisfied patients.

Objectives & Goals
Enhance patient awareness and education
Patient–dentist real-time communication
Treatment planning and collaboration for dentists
Improve post-treatment compliance

Design System

The ‘soft-touch’ interface.

Rooted in modern-corporate clinical serenity, the visual language is built to feel calm, reliable, and professional. It prioritises clarity and reassurance, acting as a quiet companion rather than a complex tool — generous whitespace, soft translucency, and high legibility. Spacing follows an 8px base unit: section gaps are wide to group content, while card padding stays generous so density never overwhelms.
