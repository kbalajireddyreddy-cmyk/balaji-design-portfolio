The Challenge

Dentists and patients weren't communicating well. Patients often didn't understand their treatment plans, which made them hesitant to move forward — and made it harder to stick with care after a procedure. That gap showed up as lower treatment compliance, more complications, and less satisfied patients.

Help patients understand their treatment
Enable real-time patient–dentist communication
Give dentists better planning tools
Improve post-treatment compliance
Process

As the only designer on this project, I ran it end to end — competitive research, personas, journey maps, sketches, wireframes, user flows, and final UI.

Competitive Analysis

I looked at three apps patients already use for dental and orthodontic care, to see what worked and what didn't.

My Invisalign
1M+ downloads · 3.6/5.0

Patients love the wear-time tracker for staying accountable — but many complained the app itself was unstable.

DentalMonitoring
500K+ downloads · 3.2/5.0

Remote check-ins save patients an office visit, and parents especially like how easily kids can use it on their own.

DentiCalc
100K+ downloads · 3.0/5.0

Its animation library helps dentists explain procedures clearly — though its pricing tools need work.

What Patients Told Us

A short patient survey surfaced three recurring asks:

  • See real-time appointment availability
  • Get automatic reminders before appointments
  • Estimate treatment costs upfront, including financing
82%
had never booked a dental appointment online
56%
said they'd prefer to pay online
The Solution
Core features
  • Appointment reminders & confirmations
  • Treatment planning tools
  • Post-operative care guidelines
Unique touches
  • A visual timeline of healing progress patients can track over time
  • An interactive walkthrough of each treatment step
  • Reminders for follow-ups, medication, and oral hygiene
Meet Sarah
Sarah, 28
Marketing Executive
The Anxious Patient

“I just want to know what's happening during the procedure so I can feel in control of my recovery.”

Sarah needs a dental implant and is anxious about the surgery and recovery. She wants to understand every step, feel in control of her healing timeline, and worry less about her clinical visits.

How It Comes Together
Patient path
Log in → Home → Check symptoms or chat with the AI assistant → Book a specialist → Confirm & pay → Track recovery
Dentist path
Log in → Dashboard → Manage patients & tasks → Plan treatment → Mark visit complete
Design System
